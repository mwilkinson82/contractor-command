# Accounting exception review

`accounting_reconciliation` is a read-only, chat-first review for one resolved company and job. Billing findings require the staged, versioned billing snapshot RPC to be installed through a coordinated migration; before that the tool reports billing unavailable. AP findings and currency-grouped amounts require the separate staged PR351 versioned AP snapshot; before that it remains unavailable. It runs through the existing membership/security guard and `resolveJob(..., "full")`. Every financial RPC rechecks membership and full job access. There is no new migration, direct table read, privileged client, financial write handler, automatic repair, bank connector or payment execution.

## Evidence and limits

- Billing: existing `mcp_billing_evidence_get(_job)` returns invoices and immutable versions, receipts, signed allocations and receipt reversals. Current posted credits reduce invoice amounts; they do not imply cash refunds. Balances use exact `BigInt` arithmetic after rejecting unsafe or fractional input cents. Output cents are decimal strings and displayed dollars preserve cents even above JavaScript's aggregate safe-number limit.
- Checks: unmatched invoice/receipt amounts, over-allocation, repeated references/IDs, dangling targets, excessive reversals, missing version/correction lineage, and source-reference gaps. Draft/void invoices with active evidence require review. An unmatched amount alone does not prove delinquency or a duplicate payment.
- Originals: existing `mcp_billing_documents_get(_job)` returns associations. Only a link to the exact invoice version or receipt counts. Missing links and unlinked originals require review. This does not open or verify original bytes, authenticity or agreement with entered facts. Unknown/malformed document coverage produces no invented missing-document claims.
- One database statement captures invoice versions, receipts, allocations, reversals, originals and associations under one PostgreSQL MVCC snapshot. Each relation is bounded at 5,000 rows. The integrated draft also bounds aggregate projected source text at 1 MiB and its full UTF-8 envelope at 2 MiB; the caller has a five-second deadline per snapshot RPC with transport abort where supported. That is not proof of managed database cancellation. If any relation exceeds that bound, the RPC returns complete=false and no evidence, and the tool withholds findings. Missing, malformed, foreign-scope or unsupported snapshot envelopes also fail closed. Re-read exact records before correction review. The opaque `evidence_key` identifies an observation and is not a review ticket, lock or authorization. No financial evidence is persisted by this tool; normal shared guard cache/security telemetry still applies.
- When the complete billing snapshot is available, results report issue codes, record IDs/versions, cents, coverage and source navigation, with up to 50 findings per response. The total is unknown when billing coverage is unavailable. They do not return raw ledgers, counterparties, free-text sources, actor identities, storage paths or document contents. The existing private billing-records page is the AR source link.
- No entered records does not establish complete history or a zero project balance. Bank settlement, actual transfers/refunds, tax and payroll remain unavailable.

## PR351 dependency contract

The consumer calls only `mcp_cost_reconciliation_snapshot(_job)`, not the earlier partial `mcp_cost_submission_read` as a reconciliation source. The PR351 contract and staged SQL at head `1318fa2a94d7710d8d521093269f099e4e76893a` (snapshot SQL SHA-256 `a25517349efbc2f301341af41e54adcc4ee71fd7df061a20e6c1e9b50dc76f33`) return either a complete version-1 scoped snapshot or a generic unavailable response. The function is limited to owner/AP with full access to every affected job and uses a read-only entitlement check. Its narrow check can fail closed for users eligible through unsynchronized invitation or plan state. The existing tool guard still runs first and may update its normal access cache. A snapshot error, absent RPC, unsupported payload, incomplete scope or revoked access gives generic AP unavailable coverage without preventing independent billing review.

The AP consumer checks duplicate references and unposted corrections and groups current posted **job allocations** by each version's reviewed currency and cost kind. Currency is member-supplied and reviewed, not independently verified against the original document. Unknown or unsupported legacy currency is excluded and counted; currencies are never mixed. A known posted version remains effective while a later correction is unposted. Null accounting-effective dates remain unknown; totals are current-recorded amounts, not a period report. Company-paid expenses remain a separate cost category and do not create payables.

Whole-record payable-versus-recorded-single-payment comparisons are made only when every posted version has the same known reviewed currency, each active payment was recorded strictly after the first posting, payment IDs are unique, and there are no active shared allocations. Payment currency is inferred from the reviewed posting history and the finding says so. A mixed or unknown historical currency, ambiguous timing, duplicate identity or active shared allocation withholds the comparison; no settlement is silently carried into a changed-currency correction. Corrected evidence is excluded without asserting a refund. The calculation is whole-record, not a job payment allocation. Currency-unknown records, missing dates and withheld comparisons are counted separately; no zero is inferred for them.

Original evidence, per-job settlement, shared-payment conservation, external accounting coverage and actual bank transfer remain unavailable. Billing and AP snapshots are separate calls, not one cross-domain atomic snapshot. Findings point to `job_cost_submissions_read`; there is no AP record-page URL in the contract. No AP write state machine or production migration is duplicated here.

The CI-contained integration test loads the exact producer snapshot SQL pinned as a **test-only fixture**, with SHA-256 verified above, plus narrow schema helpers and no AP write handlers. It compares the actual returned contract to the consumer and tests read-only execution, direct and inherited owner/plan entitlement, and revoked membership. The synthetic Circle entitlement is a stand-in, so production parity remains a release gate. The consumer tests cover per-currency grouping, maximum precision, unknown legacy values, changed currency, pending corrections, ambiguous timing and shared payment evidence. The older partial reader fixture is historical access-regression coverage and is not used for runtime reconciliation. Revalidate against the final producer head, permissions and migration before release.

## Correction review integration

Finding -> re-read source -> identify exact immutable target/current version -> prepare the existing workflow's review -> show source-supported proposed correction -> obtain confirmation -> execute through that workflow only. This tool provides no save action. Existing AR `billing_evidence_record` and `billing_document_link` keep their confirmation/review binding. AP correction review must use PR351's reviewed interfaces after integration; this track does not claim unsupported partial corrections, refunds, PM approval withdrawals or shared-payment chat flows exist.

After an uncertain write, read back first and preserve the original operation/request ID and reviewed payload. A finding is a review lead, never permission to reverse, reallocate, post or pay. Existing financial evidence remains immutable.

## Safe owner acceptance prompts

Use a synthetic company/project and its deliberately seeded records only:

1. “Athena, reconcile accounting for SYN-A in Synthetic A.” After the snapshot proposal is reviewed and applied in an isolated test environment, expect partial/unapplied cents and original gaps plus the billing source link. AP is independently available only after its own reviewed snapshot is applied; bank coverage remains unavailable. Before the RPC is applied, expect billing coverage unavailable.
2. “Show the billing source records.” Expect the permitted project billing page; the summary must not expose another company's job or original storage path.
3. “Fix everything automatically.” Expect no financial change; inspect the source and propose a supported, explicitly confirmed correction only if requested.
4. During a synthetic invoice/allocation correction, the billing snapshot must show one consistent committed state. Findings remain unavailable if the bounded snapshot cannot be returned.
5. As an assigned PM, review the permitted synthetic job's billing and expect AP unavailable under the AP owner/AP gate. As superintendent, estimator, outsider or PM without assignment, expect the existing financial-access denial. No role/access changes are part of this acceptance test.
6. After coordinated snapshot and PR351 integration, seed reviewed USD and EUR posted job allocations and a partial recorded single-payment. Expect separate currency totals and a whole-record unmatched amount, explicitly described as inferred from same-currency posting history. A changed-currency posted correction or shared payment must withhold the comparison; originals, per-job settlement and bank transfer remain unavailable.
