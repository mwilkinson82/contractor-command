# Read-only accounting evidence and accountant export

This slice adds `accounting_report` in chat and the signed-in `/r/job/<job UUID>/accounting` page, linked from Project Home for full-scope members. It reads existing billing evidence, current internal budget facts and optional AP/PO sources. It writes no financial records, schema, storage, messages, payments or access grants. The downloaded CSV is generated privately in the member's browser. No public share token grants access to this page.

## Basis and boundaries

- USD only. Existing billing RPC declares USD; inspected AP and PO drafts store USD cents without a currency field. Those legacy contracts are explicitly treated as USD. Any supplied non-USD `currency`, including nested fields, withholds the affected optional integration; non-USD billing stops the report. No currency conversion or combined currency total exists.
- Optional start and inclusive as-of dates; chat defaults to inception through the UTC capture date and asks only for a missing job. Duplicate job numbers use existing company resolution. Invalid dates, reversed ranges and future cutoffs fail.
- Billing selects the highest immutable revision whose effective date is through the cutoff, using evidence known now. This is not a reconstruction of knowledge at an earlier date. Draft/void invoices are excluded. Period gross billing and net invoice credits are reported separately by source document date. Unpaid invoices use all active net dues less credits and allocations through the cutoff, regardless of start date. Retainage is recorded withheld less released; unknown fields stay unknown. Cash uses receipt dates and reversal effective dates. Applications/reversals of applications are neither revenue nor new cash.
- AP uses the exact `posted_version`, even if there is a later unposted correction. Only explicit allocations for the requested job count as cost. Settlement evidence is separate; reimbursement is a cost once, not another cost at payment. Corrected settlement evidence is excluded from active settlement and does not assert a bank reversal. Company-paid expenses have no AP balance.
- The AP read contract omits posting events/timestamps, so historical cost and AP balances are deliberately **unknown**. Current-date costs use current posted evidence and incurred dates. Split-job settlement has no job allocation: no proportional allocation is invented, and job AP balances remain unknown for those records. AP's reader can omit split records when the actor lacks access to another affected job; consequently reporting coverage is always unverified, not a representation of all company/job liabilities.
- AP has no labor/material/sub/equipment category field. Cost kind (bill, company-paid expense, reimbursement) is not a cost category. Costs roll up by recorded cost code and are explicitly unclassified. Labor, burden, equipment, accruals and category completeness are unverified; missing coverage is never represented as zero.
- PO commitments use the highest approved/issued/accepted immutable revision created through the cutoff, and its order date. A later draft does not prove that prior approval was revoked. Internal approval does not imply external acceptance. Gross commitments are separate from costs, and are not “remaining commitments.” PO/bill matching, cancellations and externally accepted commitments require coordinated source semantics. Exact decimal line extensions are accepted only when they yield integer cents; fractional cents are unknown instead of rounded using an invented policy.
- Budget is the current known internal `mcp_job_facts` budget, not the SOV billing values, pricing estimate or CPM task cost. Stored dollar amounts convert to integer cents; unknown amounts poison the total. Comparison is current budget versus cumulative **recorded** posted costs through cutoff, not a complete variance or forecast final cost. Budget history and complete cost coverage are unverified.
- The provisional result is explicitly gross billed less net posted invoice credits less recorded posted costs in the range. It is not earned revenue, earned margin, taxable profit or a complete P&L. It is withheld when either required input is unknown. Arithmetic uses safe integer cents, with unknown on aggregate overflow.
- Source RPCs are separate reads, not an atomic close. The capture timestamp marks the start of the read. Preserve the downloaded export for the exact evidence returned; current private source URLs can change as evidence changes, and historical AP cannot be reconstructed through this contract.

## Interface contract and integration dependencies

Release base verified from remote: `1fc1bec4f60b2080743cb81e399d7c016f6981dc` (published financial documents PR #349). Do not merge or release this draft independently.

1. **Live billing**: `mcp_billing_evidence_get({_job})`; requires active membership and full job scope. Billing must succeed before the adapter reads any budget/AP/PO data. Payload: `currency: USD`, scoped `invoices[]` with immutable `versions[]`, `receipts[]`, `allocations[]`, `receipt_reversals[]`. Every row's `job_id` is checked again in the read model. The report preserves source IDs, versions, dates, financial facts and source descriptions; existing billing page provides original downloads.
2. **Live budget**: user-scoped RLS query of `mcp_job_facts` selecting `job_id,key,value,status`, exact `job_id`, `module=budget`, ordered by `key`. Pages of 500 are read to exhaustion. There is a 10,000-row cap; truncation, errors or duplicate keys fail rather than returning partial totals.
3. **Provisional AP #351**: `mcp_cost_submission_read({_job})`, full scope on the anchor and every affected job. Adapter consumes `{records:[{id,kind,posted_version,versions:[{version,facts:{kind,description,counterparty_name,incurred_on,amount_cents,allocations:[{job_id,cost_code,amount_cents}],document_reference?,purchase_order_id?,purchase_order_version?}}],payments:[{id,amount_cents,paid_on,reference,corrected,shared}]}]}`. Inspected remote draft `a4bd1869c99016f97f2faf8bb69e09426341498e` and the parent's supplied local staged source at `task-2/cost-entry/docs/athena/job-cost-submission.schema-draft.sql` (worker commit `c871a33b5435b97ed991a0e30c40ac0eeecca609`). No AP source/migration/write handler is copied or modified. Payment arrays already contain per-record shared-payment allocations; never add full shared-payment amounts again. Posting event timestamps, explicit job settlement allocations, original attachments and cost-category coverage remain source dependencies.
4. **Provisional PO #352**: `mcp_project_purchase_orders_get({_job})`, full job scope. Adapter consumes `orders:[{id,job_id,company_id,number,versions:[{version,status,created_at,order_date,lines:[{description,quantity,unit_price_cents}]}]}]`. Inspected remote draft `cef0b07f4b80c6ae7547b1bb255ecdbacf09eff4`. Company/job identity is checked when present; the browser relies on the same database full-job authorization. No terms, party contacts or default catalogs enter the report. No PO migration/write handler is modified.
5. **Optional source behavior**: only exact missing-function codes (`PGRST202`/`42883`) mean “not installed.” Schema/currency drift withholds that integration with a visible warning. Permission, membership and transport failures stop reporting, instead of producing a deceptively complete partial total. All calls use the signed-in user's client; no admin/service-role route exists.

Contract changes should be integrated by updating these adapters and synthetic contract tests. AP/PO release remains the parent's serialized migration, security/advisor and exact-head CI lane. This slice introduces **no migration** and can render billing/budget with explicit unknown optional integrations before AP/PO are installed.

## Export and pagination

The UTF-8 CSV contains rectangular rows: `type,id,date,version,label,cost_code,category,currency,amount_cents,balance_cents,basis_or_source,private_source_url`. It includes capture/basis metadata, every limitation, all summary metrics, budget comparison and the complete source ledger. Blank/unknown financial facts remain `Unknown`. Totals are not derived from a displayed page. Different row types must not be summed together.

Every cell is quoted; quotes/newlines are escaped; leading whitespace/control followed by `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` in text gets an apostrophe prefix. Numeric negative reversals remain numeric data. CSV source links require signed-in full job access and encode as-of, range, ledger page and row anchor. Original files remain behind their existing verified billing download route.

Ledger display and chat rows paginate at 50; chat cost-code rows use the same page index and include separate pagination metadata. Budget source retrieval is independently paginated, and source JSON has bounded size/row limits. Refresh can change current evidence; this is not a frozen close package.

## Coordinated snapshot requirements (PR355 owns the snapshot)

Reporting introduces no second snapshot function, table or migration. The staged `mcp_billing_reconciliation_snapshot(_job)` proposal in PR355 is the single coordinated billing/originals snapshot contract. Its current thin projection omits dates and billing/retainage amounts needed here, so this draft does not silently substitute it for the current billing reader. When its owner extends and versions the projection, both tracks can consume the same envelope. Keep full-job membership, exact company/job, bounds/completeness and omission guarantees intact.

Precise additive evidence needed by reporting:

- **Envelope:** retain `snapshot_version`, `company_id`, `job_id`, `complete`; add a database `captured_at` statement timestamp and an explicit business time zone/cutoff policy. `complete=false`, unsupported versions or scope mismatch must withhold affected totals. A billing-only snapshot does not make separately read AP, PO or budget data atomic.
- **Billing versions:** retain `invoice_id`, `job_id`, `version`, `status`, `source`, `reason`, `net_due_cents`; also return `invoice_date`, `effective_date`, `gross_cents`, `withheld_cents`, `released_cents`, and `recorded_at`. `certified_cents` and `certified_date` are needed if certification reporting is added. Return all required immutable versions or explicitly report incomplete history. Business effective date and system recorded timestamp are separate dimensions; this report currently uses effective-date evidence known now.
- **AR cash and applications:** receipts need `receipt_date` and `recorded_at`; receipt reversals need `effective_date`, `recorded_at`, `receipt_id` and amount. Allocations need `effective_date`, `recorded_at`, `receipt_id`, `invoice_id`, amount and `reversal_of`. Preserve IDs and exact job/company ownership; never derive a missing business date from a system timestamp.
- **AP posting history:** retain every immutable `(record_id, version, posted_at)` event, version `recorded_at`, and facts `incurred_on`. A newer local PR351 staged reader now includes `posting_events[{version,posted_by,posted_at}]`, but that provisional addition is not deployed, validated or consumed by this draft. A cutoff would select the latest posting effective under the explicitly agreed timestamp policy, then the matching immutable facts, never the highest unposted revision. Backdated business posting dates require a separate explicit field; do not invent them from `posted_at`.
- **AP settlement/correction timing:** preserve payment/record IDs, `paid_on`, `recorded_at`, `amount_cents`, shared flag and the exact shared payment's **per-record** allocation. Return correction ID, its original payment/shared-payment ID and `corrected_at` rather than a current boolean alone. A correction entered after a historical cutoff cannot erase earlier evidence from that cutoff. Corrections remain evidence corrections, not proof of bank refunds or transfers. Company/job envelopes and omitted split-record counts/completeness are required before claiming complete AP coverage.
- **Project settlement allocation:** to report a split bill's job AP balance or job cash paid, supply immutable, conserved settlement allocations carrying payment ID, record ID/version, `job_id`, amount and currency, plus effective/recorded dates and correction lineage. For cost-code settlement reporting also supply `cost_code`. Current facts allocations allocate **cost**, not payment; they cannot be repurposed or prorated. Shared headers need their authorized total and authorized per-record/per-job allocations so a header is not summed again. Continue the all-affected-jobs gate; if that hides records, explicitly disclose incomplete coverage without exposing hidden job details.

Cost-category completeness, labor/burden/equipment sources, historical budgets, PO cancellations/approval timing and bill-to-PO matching remain separate source design decisions. This draft continues to withhold unsupported historical AP/cost and split-settlement totals until coordinated contracts are applied and tested. It does not infer completeness from matching repeated reads.
