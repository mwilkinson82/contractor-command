## Article 1 – Contract Documents

1.1 The Subcontract Documents consist of this Agreement, Exhibit A (Scope of Work), Exhibit B (Schedule of Values), the Prime Contract between Owner and Contractor so far as it applies to the Work, and the Drawings, Specifications (Divisions 26, 27 and 28) and Addenda issued before execution.

## Article 2 – Scope of Work

2.1 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, permits and inspections necessary to complete the electrical work described in Exhibit A, complete and operational, in accordance with the Subcontract Documents and the NEC as adopted by the City of Corpus Christi.

## Article 3 – Payment

3.1 **Payment Applications.** Subcontractor shall submit its application for payment on or before the **20th day of each month** for Work performed through the end of that month, on **AIA G702/G703 format**, itemized against the approved **Schedule of Values (Exhibit B)**.

3.2 **Time of Payment.** Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the amount approved, less retainage, within **ten (10) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner** for the Work covered by the application.

3.3 **Notice of Non-Payment.** If payment is not received when due under Section 3.2, Subcontractor shall give Contractor **written notice of non-payment within ten (10) days of the missed due date**, delivered by email to Contractor's Project Manager with a copy by certified mail. Timely written notice is a **condition precedent** to any claim for payment, interest or suspension of Work arising from the late payment.

## Article 4 – Retainage

4.1 Contractor shall withhold **ten percent (10%)** of each progress payment until the Work is **fifty percent (50%) complete**, and **five percent (5%)** thereafter, provided Subcontractor's performance is satisfactory.

4.2 Retainage shall be released **thirty (30) days after Owner's final acceptance** of the Project and Subcontractor's delivery of all closeout documents required by Article 9 and a final unconditional lien waiver.

## Article 5 – Submittals

5.1 Subcontractor shall submit all shop drawings, product data and samples **within twenty-one (21) calendar days after Notice to Proceed**. Submittals for **switchgear, switchboards, panelboards and the generator shall be submitted within ten (10) calendar days after Notice to Proceed**.

5.2 Subcontractor shall allow **fourteen (14) calendar days for Architect review** of each submittal.

## Article 6 – Schedule

6.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Work in accordance with **Contractor's CPM schedule** as updated, and shall submit a **two-week lookahead schedule weekly**.

6.2 **Delay Notice.** Subcontractor shall give written notice of any delay **within five (5) calendar days** after the start of the event, stating the cause and the estimated impact, delivered by email to Contractor's Project Manager. Failure to give timely notice waives the claim.

6.3 **Liquidated Damages.** If Owner assesses liquidated damages under the Prime Contract, Contractor may pass those damages through to Subcontractor in proportion to Subcontractor's responsibility, at **$1,500 per calendar day**.

## Article 7 – Changes and Claims

7.1 No change in the Work shall be performed without a **written Change Order or written directive** from Contractor, except in an emergency endangering life or property.

7.2 Subcontractor shall submit an itemized price for any requested change **within seven (7) calendar days** of Contractor's request.

7.3 **Claim Notice.** Any claim for additional time or money must be submitted in writing **within ten (10) calendar days of the event** giving rise to it, delivered by email to Contractor's Project Manager with supporting documentation. Claims not timely noticed are waived.

## Article 8 – Warranty

8.1 **Workmanship Warranty.** Subcontractor warrants that all Work will be free of defects in materials and workmanship for **one (1) year from the date of Substantial Completion** of the Project, or longer where the Specifications require. Subcontractor shall correct defective Work promptly upon notice at no cost.

## Article 9 – Closeout

9.1 Before final payment, Subcontractor shall deliver: **operation and maintenance (O&M) manuals**, **as-built drawings**, **Owner training** sessions with sign-in sheets, manufacturer warranties for the switchgear, generator and lighting fixtures, test reports and permit/inspection sign-offs.

## Article 10 – Termination

10.1 **For Cause.** If Subcontractor fails to perform and does not cure within three (3) working days after written notice, Contractor may terminate or supplement the Work and charge the cost to Subcontractor.
