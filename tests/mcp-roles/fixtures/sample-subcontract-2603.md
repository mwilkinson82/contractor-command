## Article 1 – Contract Documents

1.1 The Subcontract Documents consist of this Agreement, Exhibit A (Scope of Work), Exhibit B (Schedule of Values), the Prime Contract between Owner and Contractor so far as it applies to the Work, and the Drawings, Specifications (including Division 26, 27 and 28) and Addenda issued before execution. Subcontractor is bound to Contractor to the same extent Contractor is bound to Owner for the Work.

1.2 In case of conflict, the more stringent requirement governs, and the greater quantity or higher quality controls unless Contractor directs otherwise in writing.

## Article 2 – Scope of Work

2.1 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, tools, permits, inspections, and services necessary to complete the electrical work described in Exhibit A, complete and operational, in accordance with the Subcontract Documents and all applicable codes, including the NEC as adopted by the City of Austin.

## Article 3 – Subcontract Sum and Payment

3.1 **Payment Applications.** Subcontractor shall submit its application for payment on or before the **25th day of each month** for Work performed through the end of that month, on **AIA G702/G703 format**, itemized against the approved **Schedule of Values (Exhibit B)**. Applications received after the 25th will be included in the following month's application to Owner.

3.2 **Lien Waivers.** Each application shall be accompanied by a conditional lien waiver for the current payment and an unconditional lien waiver for the previous payment, in the statutory form required by Texas Property Code Chapter 53, together with lower-tier supplier waivers when requested.

3.3 **Time of Payment.** Contractor shall pay Subcontractor the amount approved, less retainage, within **seven (7) days after Contractor receives payment from Owner** for the Work covered by the application.

3.4 **Notice of Non-Payment.** If payment is not received when due under Section 3.3, Subcontractor shall give Contractor **written notice of non-payment within ten (10) days of the missed due date**. Timely written notice is a **condition precedent** to any claim for payment, interest or suspension of Work arising from the late payment. This notice does not replace any statutory notice required to preserve lien or bond rights.

3.5 Contractor may withhold payment for defective Work not remedied, third-party claims, failure to pay lower tiers, failure to maintain the schedule, or failure to provide required submittals, lien waivers or reports.

## Article 4 – Retainage

4.1 Contractor shall withhold **ten percent (10%)** of each progress payment until the Work is **fifty percent (50%) complete**. Thereafter, retainage on subsequent payments shall be **five percent (5%)**, provided Subcontractor's performance is satisfactory.

4.2 Retainage shall be released **thirty (30) days after Owner's final acceptance** of the Project and Subcontractor's delivery of all **closeout documents** required by Article 9, a final unconditional lien waiver, and consent of surety if applicable.

## Article 5 – Submittals

5.1 Subcontractor shall submit all shop drawings, product data, and samples **within twenty-one (21) calendar days after Notice to Proceed**.

5.2 Submittals for **long-lead equipment, including switchgear, switchboards and panelboards, shall be submitted within ten (10) calendar days after Notice to Proceed**.

5.3 Subcontractor shall allow **fourteen (14) calendar days for Architect review** of each submittal. Rejected or "revise and resubmit" submittals shall be **resubmitted within seven (7) calendar days** after return.

5.4 Delays caused by late or incomplete submittals are Subcontractor's responsibility. Approval of a submittal does not relieve Subcontractor of conformance with the Subcontract Documents.

## Article 6 – Schedule

6.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Work in accordance with **Contractor's CPM schedule** as updated, and shall furnish activity durations, sequencing and resource information when requested.

6.2 Subcontractor shall submit a **three-week lookahead schedule weekly** and a **daily manpower report** listing workers by classification and area.

6.3 Contractor will issue **schedule updates every Friday**. Subcontractor shall review each update and promptly notify Contractor of any disagreement.

6.4 **Delay Notice.** Subcontractor shall give written notice of any delay **within three (3) calendar days** after the start of the event, stating the cause and the estimated impact. Failure to give timely notice waives the claim.

6.5 **No Damages for Delay.** Subcontractor's sole remedy for delay, hindrance or disruption, regardless of cause, shall be an **extension of time only**, and Subcontractor shall have no claim for damages or additional compensation.

6.6 **Liquidated Damages.** If Owner assesses liquidated damages under the Prime Contract, Contractor may pass those damages through to Subcontractor in proportion to Subcontractor's responsibility, at **$1,000 per calendar day**.

## Article 7 – Changes and Claims

7.1 No change in the Work shall be performed without a **written Change Order or written directive** from Contractor, **except in an emergency** endangering life or property. Work performed without written authorization is at Subcontractor's own risk.

7.2 Subcontractor shall submit an itemized price for any requested change **within seven (7) calendar days** of Contractor's request.

7.3 Markup on change order work is limited to **ten percent (10%) overhead and five percent (5%) profit** on Subcontractor's direct cost of labor, material and equipment.

7.4 **Claim Notice.** Any claim for additional time or money must be submitted in writing **within seven (7) calendar days of the event** giving rise to it. Claims not timely noticed are waived.

7.5 **Time and Material Tickets.** T&M work shall be recorded on tickets **signed by Contractor's superintendent the same day** the work is performed. Unsigned tickets will not be paid.

## Article 8 – Warranty

8.1 Subcontractor warrants that all Work will be free of defects in materials and workmanship for **one (1) year from Owner's acceptance** of the Project, or longer where the Specifications require. Subcontractor shall correct defective Work promptly upon notice at no cost.

## Article 9 – Closeout

9.1 Before final payment, Subcontractor shall deliver: **operation and maintenance (O&M) manuals**, **as-built drawings**, **attic stock** as specified, **Owner training** sessions with sign-in sheets, manufacturer warranties, test reports and permit/inspection sign-offs.

## Article 10 – Insurance and Indemnity

10.1 Subcontractor shall maintain Commercial General Liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate, Automobile Liability of $1,000,000, Workers' Compensation at statutory limits, and Umbrella of $2,000,000, naming Contractor and Owner as additional insureds on a primary, non-contributory basis with waiver of subrogation.

10.2 To the fullest extent permitted by law, Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Contractor and Owner from claims arising from Subcontractor's negligent acts or omissions in performing the Work, subject to Texas Insurance Code Chapter 151.

## Article 11 – Termination

11.1 **For Cause.** If Subcontractor fails to perform and does not cure within three (3) working days after written notice, Contractor may terminate or supplement the Work and charge the cost to Subcontractor.

11.2 **For Convenience.** Contractor may terminate for convenience on written notice; Subcontractor shall be paid for Work performed plus reasonable demobilization costs, without anticipated profit on unperformed Work.

## Article 12 – Disputes

12.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through negotiation between project managers, then senior officers. Unresolved disputes shall go to mediation in Travis County, Texas, and then to litigation in the courts of Travis County. Subcontractor shall continue the Work pending resolution. This Agreement is governed by Texas law.
